Pricing

Prices are estimated based on the tattoo’s design, placement, size, level of detail, and required session time.

  • Starting price: $100
  • Custom tattoos: from $200
  • Touch-ups: one free touch-up within 3 months, then $50 after 3 months
Deposits

A deposit is required to secure all appointments and will be deducted from the final tattoo price.

  • Flash designs: 50% of the listed or quoted price
  • Custom tattoos: from $200
  • Promotional designs: full promotional or base amount
  • Large pieces: 50% of the estimated total

Good to know

  • The remaining balance is due on the day of your appointment.
  • Changes to placement, size, concept, or design details may affect the final quote.
  • Numbing cream is available at an additional $30–$50 if arranged in advance.
Payment

Payment details are sent together with your quote, once your request has been approved.

PayNow & bank transfer

  • Pay by PayNow (QR code or UEN) or bank transfer to a POSB account.
  • Please send a screenshot of the successful transaction after payment.

GrabPay / GrabPayLater

Option 1: pay the deposit through PayNow, then the remaining balance through GrabPay or GrabPayLater on the appointment day, plus a 5% admin fee.

Example: $300 tattoo with a $100 deposit → $200 balance + $10 fee = $210 on the day

Option 2: pay the full confirmed price through GrabPay or GrabPayLater before your appointment, plus a 5% admin fee.

Example: $300 tattoo → $300 + $15 fee = $315 before the appointment

Once a GrabPay or GrabPayLater payment has been made, no refunds will be issued for cancellation or change of mind. If changes to the design, subject, placement, or size affect the final price, any extra amount must be topped up on the appointment day through PayNow or GrabPay / GrabPayLater.

Appointment policies

Lateness

  • More than 15 minutes late: $50 late fee
  • More than 30 minutes late: appointment cancelled and the deposit is forfeited
  • A new deposit is required before rebooking

Cancellation & rescheduling

  • Deposits are non-refundable once confirmed
  • Give at least 48 hours’ notice to reschedule
  • Late cancellations, no-shows, and missed appointments forfeit the deposit
  • New deposits are required where applicable

Design changes

  • Quotes are estimates, not fixed promises
  • Major changes after drafting may affect the final price
  • A revised design, placement, size, or concept may require a new deposit

Studio etiquette

  • Please respect the studio’s shoe-free areas
  • Clients or guests who enter with shoes may incur a cleaning fee — please confirm the amount with the artist before your appointment
Preparing for your tattoo

The days before

  • No alcohol or recreational drugs for 36 hours before your appointment. If you take blood-thinning medication, check with your doctor first — please don’t stop prescribed medication on your own.
  • Avoid swimming and tanning for 24 hours before, and keep the area free from sunburn.
  • Get a good night’s sleep.
  • Let the artist know in advance if you are considering numbing cream.

On the day

  • Eat a proper meal beforehand and stay hydrated.
  • Wear comfortable clothing that gives easy access to the tattoo placement.
  • Please arrive on time.
  • Getting tattooed during menstruation may be more painful for some clients.
  • By booking, you consent to photos and videos being taken for the artist’s portfolio and social media.

If you have a medical condition, take prescription medication, are unsure about a health-related risk, or have concerns about healing, seek appropriate medical advice before booking.
