05

Consent & preparation

By booking, you acknowledge the tattoo consent and waiver terms.

!

If you have a medical condition, take prescription medication, are unsure about a health-related risk, or have concerns about healing, seek appropriate medical advice before booking.

Before your appointment ...

Do not consume alcohol, recreational drugs, or blood thinners for 36 hours before your appointment.

Avoid swimming and tanning for 24 hours before your appointment.

Getting tattooed during menstruation may be more painful for some clients.

Let the artist know in advance if you are considering numbing cream.

By booking, you agree that ...

You understand tattooing carries risks, including infection, scarring, keloids, allergic reactions, and autoimmune flare-ups.

You consent to photos and videos being taken for the artist’s portfolio and social media.

You are responsible for following the artist’s aftercare instructions.

One complimentary touch-up is included within 3 months; touch-ups after 3 months are $50.

GrabPay / GrabPayLater

There are two kinds of GrabPay options you can use for GrabPay payments:

Once a GrabPay or GrabPayLater payment has been made, no refunds will be issued for cancellation or change of mind. If the tattoo design, subject, placement, or size changes and affects the final quoted price, any additional amount must be topped up on the appointment day through PayNow or GrabPay / GrabPayLater.

Option 1:

Deposit via PayNow, balance via GrabPayLater

Pay the deposit through PayNow first. Pay the remaining tattoo balance through GrabPay or GrabPayLater on the appointment day, plus the 5% administration fee.

Example: $300 tattoo with a $100 deposit

Remaining appointment balance: $300 − $100 = $200
5% GrabPayLater admin fee: $200 × 5% = $10
GrabPayLater due on appointment day: $210

Option 2:

Full amount via GrabPayLater before appointment

Pay the full confirmed tattoo price through GrabPay or GrabPayLater before your appointment, plus the 5% administration fee.

Example: $300 tattoo paid in full

Total tattoo amount: $300
5% GrabPayLater admin fee: $300 × 5% = $15
GrabPayLater amount due before appointment: $315
