Second Skin

Leave it on for 2–3 days.

It is normal to see some blood, plasma, or excess ink collecting beneath the film. There is no need to worry.

Remove the Second Skin immediately if you notice irritation, redness, or itching around the edges.

If water gets underneath the film or the film starts to come off, that is okay. Remove it and do not replace it with another adhesive bandage.

To remove it, do so under running water. Gently stretch and pull the film downwards along the skin, similar to removing a 3M Command strip. Do not pull it straight away from the skin.

After removal, wash the tattoo gently with mild soap and water.

Cleaning & hygiene

Once you are home, wash the tattoo gently with mild soap and water.

You may shower as normal. Pat the tattoo dry gently with a clean towel or paper towel—do not rub.

Avoid very hot water, heavy scrubbing, and excessive soap directly on the tattoo, as this can dry and irritate the skin.

Daily upkeep

Moisturise your tattoo a few times per day using a thin layer only. Do not leave excess product sitting on the skin.

Suitable options include tattoo balm, Bepanthen, or a gentle unscented moisturiser.

Avoid swimming, saunas, hot tubs, and prolonged direct sunlight for 4 weeks.

Avoid strenuous exercise and excessive sweating while the tattoo is healing.

Avoid alcohol, seafood, and spicy food for the first week if they may affect your healing or cause irritation.

Healing reminders

Some tattoos may scab during healing. If this happens, let the scab fall off naturally.

Do not pick, peel, or scratch your tattoo.

For long-term care, moisturise regularly and use sunscreen when your tattoo will be exposed to the sun.
